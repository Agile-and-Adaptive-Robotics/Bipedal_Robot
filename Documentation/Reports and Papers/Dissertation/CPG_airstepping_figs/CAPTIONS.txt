CPG air-stepping figures for the dissertation - generated 2026-09-11
=====================================================================

Simulation configuration for ALL figures:
  Full 92-muscle OpenSim gait2392 model converted to MuJoCo, driven by a
  synthetic spinal cord (406 non-spiking neurons: bilateral RG half-centers
  with slow adaptation, 4 phase-grouped PF cells per side, 92 motoneuron
  pools). Deafferented (no proprioceptive feedback), no interleg loading,
  body suspended (no ground contact), trunk clamped. Descending drive
  1.5 nA -> cycle period ~1.7 s (0.58 Hz), within the instructed range for
  human air-stepping (Ivanenko et al. 2002: preferred 0.3 Hz; ~2x slower
  than grounded walking). Joint ranges: hip -16..+58 deg, knee -85..+16 deg
  (flexion negative, OpenSim convention).
