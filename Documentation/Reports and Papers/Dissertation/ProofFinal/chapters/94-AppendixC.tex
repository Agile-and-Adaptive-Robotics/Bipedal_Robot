\chapter{Appendix C: Model Geometry, Bracket Frames, and Compliance Parameters}\label{app:model_geometry}
\graphicspath{{figs/AppendixC/}}

This appendix documents the numeric geometry and compliance parameters behind the torque models of Chapters~\ref{ch:methods}--\ref{ch:results} as they exist in the project's MATLAB code, with file names given so that any value can be traced to the code that produced it. Route and bracket coordinates are coded in meters and reported here in millimeters; stiffnesses are coded and reported in newtons per meter. The bracket reference points are interpretation choices for the 3D-printed (Onyx FDM) attachment hardware, set from CAD by the author; moving a reference point changes the identified stiffness values, so the points are recorded here alongside the values they produced.

\section{Static BPA Pathway Data}

The static BPA model routes each actuator as a straight-segment polyline through via points stored in a per-test \texttt{Location} array of dimensions $M\times3\times N$, where $N$ is the number of joint frames in the motion sweep and $M$ the number of via points; the first row in the distal link frame is marked by a cross-point index. There are no binormal or surface-frame quantities: at each frame the force direction is the normalized vector from the cross-point row to the previous row transformed into the crossing frame,
\begin{equation}
    \hat{\mathbf{u}} \;\propto\; \mathbf{R}^{-1}\,\mathbf{p}_{C-1} \;-\; \mathbf{p}_{C},
    \label{eq:app_forcedir}
\end{equation}
where $\mathbf{p}_{C}$ is the cross-point row, $\mathbf{p}_{C-1}$ the previous row, and $\mathbf{R}$ the frame-crossing rotation [Provenance: \texttt{minimizeFlxPin.m} lines 160 and 524--537 (\texttt{UD}) and 201--235 (\texttt{computeForceVector}); \texttt{minimizeExtX3.m} lines 687--700; BPA struct fields, \texttt{minimizeFlxPin.m} lines 28--35].

\section{Joint Transformation Matrices}

The knee transform is planar: every joint frame is a rotation about $z$ by the knee angle $\varphi$ composed with a translation fitted to robot CAD tables of the migrating instantaneous center of rotation. With $\mathbf{R} = \mathbf{R}_{z}(\varphi)$, the robot (femur$\to$ICR) transform is $\mathbf{T}_{\text{Pam}} = [\mathbf{R},\; [f_{3}(\varphi),\,f_{4}(\varphi),\,0]^{\top};\; \mathbf{0}^{\top}\;1]$ with $f_{3}, f_{4}$ cubic-spline fits (ICR $x$: 23.30 to 10.47~mm; $y$: $-416.65$ to $-395.66$~mm over $\varphi = 0.17$ to $-2.62$~rad), and the ICR$\to$tibia transform is the pure translation $[f_{13}(\varphi),\,f_{14}(\varphi),\,0]^{\top}$ ($x$: 29.66 to $-7.58$~mm; $y$: 25.97 to 22.33~mm), composed as $\mathbf{T}_{t1\!\to\!f} = \mathbf{T}_{t1\!\to\!\text{ICR}}\;\mathbf{T}_{\text{Pam}}^{-1}$ over 100 frames spanning $\varphi \in [-120^{\circ}, +10^{\circ}]$ [Provenance: \texttt{buildKneeFlexorContext20mm.m} lines 34--80; \texttt{buildKneeExtContext20mm.m} lines 30--78; \texttt{Functions/ModernRobotics/RpToTrans.m}]. In the archived test sets the per-frame transform maps tibia to femur/ICR; at $\theta_{k}=-120^{\circ}$ (test kf1),
\begin{equation}
    \mathbf{T}_{k} \;=\;
    \begin{bmatrix}
    -0.5000 & 0.8660 & 0 & 0.0045\\
    -0.8660 & -0.5000 & 0 & -0.3958\\
    0 & 0 & 1 & 0\\
    0 & 0 & 0 & 1
    \end{bmatrix},
    \label{eq:app_Tk}
\end{equation}
i.e., $\mathbf{R}_{z}(-120^{\circ})$ with constant translation $[4.5,\,-395.8,\,0]$~mm, rotating to $\mathbf{R}_{z}(+10^{\circ})$ at full extension (\texttt{FlxPinBPASet.mat}, kf(1)).

\section{Bracket Reference Points and Frames}

Table~\ref{tab:app_bracket_points} lists the bracket reference points. Each compliant bracket carries an instantaneous body-fixed frame anchored at its reference point, constructed by two rotations (yaw about $z$, then pitch about the yawed $y$). With $\mathbf{p}_{b}$ the vector from the reference point to the bracket-to-BPA attachment target, the yaw and pitch angles are
\begin{equation}
    \theta_{br} = \operatorname{atan2}(p_{by},\, p_{bx}),
    \qquad
    \phi_{br} = \operatorname{atan2}(p'_{bz},\, p'_{bx})
    \quad\text{with}\quad
    \mathbf{p}'_{b} = \mathbf{R}_{z}(\theta_{br})^{\top}\mathbf{p}_{b},
    \label{eq:app_bracketangles}
\end{equation}
and the bracket-frame pose is
\begin{equation}
    \mathbf{R}_{kbr} = \mathbf{R}_{z}(\theta_{br})\,\mathbf{R}_{y}(\phi_{br})^{\top},
    \qquad
    \mathbf{T}_{kbr} = \left[\mathbf{R}_{kbr},\; \mathbf{p}_{br};\; \mathbf{0}\;\,1\right],
    \label{eq:app_bracketframe}
\end{equation}
so the frame's $x$ axis aims along the nominal load path. The angles are computed per configuration from the bracket geometry, not fixed [Provenance: \texttt{minimizeFlxPin2brk.m} lines 252--298; \texttt{minimizeExtX3.m} lines 461--483]. As executed, however, the flexor evaluator of record assembles \emph{both} of its bracket frames from the yaw rotation alone (the pitch-only, one-transform pose $\mathbf{T}_{kbr} = [\mathbf{R}_{z}(\theta_{br}), \mathbf{p}_{br}; \mathbf{0}\;1]$): insertion bracket at line 289 and origin bracket at line 305 of \texttt{minimizeFlxPin.m}, with the matching pitch-only point reconstructions at lines 318 and 331; the two-rotation assembly of Eq.~\eqref{eq:app_bracketframe} is computed but left commented at lines 288, 304, and 330. For the pinned extensor the two-rotation assembly is live (transMode default \texttt{2trans}; \texttt{minimizeExtX3.m} lines 3--6 and 479--482). Force is transformed into the bracket frame by $\mathbf{RowVecTrans}(\mathbf{T}_{kbr}^{-1}, \mathbf{F}_{k})$ and deformed points are mapped back by $\mathbf{RowVecTrans}(\mathbf{T}_{kbr}, \cdot)$. Because the insertion array $[X_{1},X_{2},X_{1}]$ is $y$-symmetric, the assembly choice is numerically immaterial there; for the origin bracket it is not (see Table~\ref{tab:app_stiffness_arrays}). The pitch-only (yaw-only) variant that the earlier one-transform fit used survives in \texttt{minimizeFlxPinX3.m} lines 200--209.

\section{Stiffness Arrays and Compliance}

Every configuration models bracket compliance as a diagonal Hooke-law spring in the bracket frame, $\mathbf{K}_{br} = \operatorname{diag}(k_{1}, k_{2}, k_{3})$ newtons per meter. The scalar compliance along the force path and its series combinations are
\begin{equation}
    c_{b} = \hat{\mathbf{u}}\,\mathbf{K}_{br}^{-1}\hat{\mathbf{u}}^{\top},
    \qquad
    c_{\text{eff}} = c_{b} + c_{\text{ten}} \;(+\,c_{b2}\ \text{for the second bracket}),
    \label{eq:app_compliance}
\end{equation}
exactly as in Equations~(\ref{eq:bktstiffness})--(\ref{eq:keff}); when an artificial tendon is present its axial spring ($\times2$ cables, effective steel area $1.51\times10^{-6}$~m$^{2}$, $E = 193$~GPa) adds in series, and the force balance $F_{\max}F^{*}(\varepsilon^{*}(\delta), P^{*}) = k_{\text{eff}}\,\delta$ is solved per frame by \texttt{fzero} on the contraction interval [Provenance: stiffness arrays --- \texttt{minimizeFlxPin.m} lines 387 and 400; \texttt{minimizeExtX3.m} lines 575--584; \texttt{minimizeExt.m} line 352; \texttt{minimizeFlx.m} line 314. \texttt{fzero} force balance --- \texttt{minimizeFlxPin.m} line 438; \texttt{minimizeExtX3.m} line 611; \texttt{minimizeExt.m} line 389; \texttt{minimizeFlx.m} line 352]. The per-configuration stiffness arrays and their series combinations are given in Table~\ref{tab:app_stiffness_arrays}.

\section{Adopted Correction-Term Values}

Table~\ref{tab:app_xi_values} records the correction-term values adopted at the time of writing, together with the results file and front row each value was taken from. The initial single-bracket identification reported with the results figures (Table~\ref{tab:OptCoeff}) was superseded by the two-stage campaign described in Section~\ref{sec:ongoing}: a two-bracket flexor identification over the four training tests of Table~\ref{tab:app_flxpin_tests} (encoder-corrected, cross-validated), followed by an extensor refit that locks $(X_{1}, X_{2})$ to a member of the same flexor Pareto front and solves only $(X_{0}, X_{3})$ on the extensor validation pool. The pinned-flexor data do not uniquely determine $(X_{1}, X_{2})$ --- the cross-validation fronts contain a family of stiffness pairs of comparable fit quality --- so the adopted flexor row (front row 77) and the locked extensor refit (flxr77 front row 32, generated with the pair fixed to that same row-77 pair) are two operating points tied to one and the same stiffness pair. The row-77 pair was adopted because it is the pair for which both redesign configurations meet the required 5\,\% torque-overshoot target simultaneously: in the 2026-09-20 campaign the flexor redesign reached a minimum margin of $+5.00$\,\% and the locked extensor redesign $+5.043$\,\% at $-20.2^{\circ}$ (\texttt{Dig\_out/frommat\_flx\_20260920.log} and \texttt{Dig\_out/Opt\_flxr77\_20260920.log}); the refined redesigns of record improve this to $+5.01$\,\% (flexor, 2026-09-21) and $+5.03$\,\% (extensor, 2026-09-25).

At the adopted flexor values, per-test torque-prediction RMSE against the five pinned-flexor tests is 2.16/1.37/2.29/1.61/1.08~N{\,m} (48, 46, 47, 40-tendon, 41\,cm; FVU 0.17/0.05/0.16/0.04/0.03), the 47\,cm test being pure held-out validation. At the adopted extensor values, per-test RMSE on the six-test validation pool spans 0.49--1.52~N{\,m} against 0.95--3.62~N{\,m} for the uncorrected rigid model, improving on the baseline in every pool test; the fraction of variance unexplained stays below unity on every pool test except the 43\,cm-tendon test (FVU 1.33, against a rigid-baseline FVU of 4.73). The three excluded tests (3, 4, 9) are not part of the fit of record. On the biomimetic extensor test ($l_{0} = 51.8$~cm) the same values give RMSE 1.17~N{\,m}, FVU 0.67, and maximum residual 2.21~N{\,m}, against a rigid baseline of 3.03/4.49/5.59 --- the shared-pair correction now explains the bulk of that configuration's measured torque variance as well as its level. The redesign optimizations load these values directly: \texttt{buildKneeFlexorContext20mm.m} reads the flexor front, pick 77, for $(X_{0},X_{1},X_{2})$ at lines 171--182 (its $X_{3}$ from flxr77 row 32 at lines 188--191), and \texttt{buildKneeExtContext20mm.m} reads the flxr77 extensor results file, pick 32, at lines 164--171. Regenerating the results files updates this table.
