Two-panel schematic FEA illustration of a pneumatic artificial muscle. Panel A shows an exploded upper-half cutaway with fixed metal end cap, rubber layers, opposing aramid helices, and fabric covering; the symmetry-plane callout points to a translucent plane rather than material. Panel B shows six idealized wall layers, outside to inside: fabric outer, outer rubber, aramid helix, middle rubber, opposite-handed inner helix, and inner rubber. Internal air pressure acts outward on the inner wall, and contact-interface symbols indicate interlayer FEA contact. A fixed end, longitudinal symmetry plane, radial-expansion inset, half-length boundary, optional axial load, and alternative axial fixed or movable boundary conditions are shown. Geometry, material separation, and contact glyphs are illustrative rather than a solver-ready mesh.
