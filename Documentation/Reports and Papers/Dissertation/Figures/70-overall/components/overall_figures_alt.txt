Alt text for the Chapter 7 figures (generated by make_overall_figures.py).

design_workflow.pdf — Flow diagram of the first-pass design workflow. Two
input boxes on the left, Human torque and range-of-motion targets (OpenSim
benchmark) above BPA force model (Ch. 3), feed Select actuator set, diameter,
and resting length, which feeds Route design under the corrected torque model
(Ch. 4). The predicted torque and range of motion enter a Meets targets?
diamond; on yes the flow proceeds to Commit to hardware, and from there down
to the dashed box Closed-loop verification in simulation (Ch. 6); on no a
return arrow loops back to route design labeled "no: redesign route or
reselect actuator".

sensorium_hierarchy.pdf — Three labeled tiers. Top tier, Supraspinal
additions (future work), three dashed boxes: Basal ganglia (action and mode
selection); Cerebellar adaptive gateway (gait gating and phase tuning);
Vestibular and ocular sensing (IMU orientation, visual drift correction). A
descending-commands arrow reaches the middle tier, Spinal network
(implemented, Ch. 6): Two-level CPG (rhythm generator and pattern formation)
beside Motor-neuron pools with Ia, Ib, and II afferents and heel and toe
contact. Activation arrows reach the bottom tier, Body (demonstrated): BPA
actuators and robot body, with the balance-platform precursor of the
vestibular channel noted inside; proprioceptive and contact feedback returns
upward, and a future arrow returns body orientation and vision to the
vestibular and ocular box.

bpa_construction.pdf — Schematic longitudinal section of a braided pneumatic
actuator. An elastomer bladder sits inside a braided aramid sleeve drawn with
two handed crisscross weaves, sealed at each end by a gray Onyx or aluminum
end cap. Leader labels mark the sleeve, the bladder, and a cap; a dashed
horizontal symmetry axis runs through the actuator. Not to scale.
