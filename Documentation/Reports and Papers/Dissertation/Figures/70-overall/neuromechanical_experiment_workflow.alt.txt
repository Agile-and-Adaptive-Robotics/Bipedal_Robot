A neural controller with specified motor outputs and sensory pathways is paired with a verified Gait2392, rerouted robot, or CAD-derived mechanical model. Motor commands go to the muscles, and sensed signals return to the circuit. The baseline sets the initial pose, stimulus, descending drive, and support for either of two independent repeats. In the sensory repeat, one pathway changes while the model, actuator routes, and other neural settings stay fixed. In the mechanical repeat, one attachment or route changes while the controller and sensory settings stay fixed. Each repeat is aligned with the baseline at stimulus onset or a comparable foot-contact event. Neural activity, muscle force, joint motion, and foot loads reveal the first recorded difference.
