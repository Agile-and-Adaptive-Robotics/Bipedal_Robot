(A) Each Ia sensory neuron excites its corresponding extensor or flexor motor neuron and inhibits the antagonist. Ib input inhibits its corresponding motor neuron. The two actuator drawings show pennate human muscles. (B) A 2 nA current pulse is applied to extensor Ia for 30 ms. (C) Extensor motor-neuron voltage rises and flexor motor-neuron voltage falls. (D) Muscle activation and (E) force changes are shown for each side. (F) Knee extension peaks at 0.936 degrees at 167 ms. Changes are relative to the matched unstimulated model. The time-series panels share time zero at pulse onset in the detailed and longer views.
