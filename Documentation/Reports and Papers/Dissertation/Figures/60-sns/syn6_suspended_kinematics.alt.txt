Left and right hips, knees, and ankles of suspended syn6 are compared with recorded Subject01 walking and walking simulated by Ong and colleagues. Light curves are the seven individual suspended cycles. The blue mean knee excursions are 19 degrees on the left and 34 degrees on the right, against about 70 to 72 degrees in the reference curves. All joints share the time origin of maximum right-knee flexion. Beneath each leg, dark bars mark reference stance and pale gray bars mark swing. Neither suspended foot carried load. Knee flexion is negative.
