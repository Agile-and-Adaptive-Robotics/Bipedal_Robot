Alt text for sns_gui_demo_circuit.png

Screenshot of the SNSGui application with three panels. On the left, a
palette lists the fifteen SNS_Library blocks from the graded neuron to
the hybrid spiking synapse. In the center, a canvas shows a reduced
knee-reflex circuit: five graded-neuron circles (two motoneurons on the
right, three sensory interneurons in the middle), two Ia spindle capsules
and one Ib tendon capsule on the left, and one McKibben muscle glyph at
the bottom; afferent edges drawn as current injections with small dots,
and interneuron-to-motoneuron connections drawn with white triangles for
excitation and solid black dots for inhibition, each routed with right-
angle segments. One excitatory connection is selected and outlined in
blue. On the right, a property panel titled with the selected connection
shows editable numeric fields for gmax, Esyn, ThrPre, and SlopePre. A
status bar across the bottom reports the loaded circuit and a timing.
