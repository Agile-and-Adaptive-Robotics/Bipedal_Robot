Alt text for sns_gui_production_network.png

Screenshot of the SNSGui application after loading the tuned production
spinal-network export. The palette on the left is unchanged. The canvas
shows 410 graded-neuron circles arranged in five labeled category columns
from left to right (shared and drive cells, rhythm-generator and
adaptation cells, pattern-formation cells, sensory interneurons, and
motoneurons), with 1392 connections drawn as thin lines with white
triangles or black dots by sign; the density of crossing wires conveys
the scale of the network. The property panel shows the selection prompt,
and the status bar reports 410 neurons, 1392 synapses, and the load time.
