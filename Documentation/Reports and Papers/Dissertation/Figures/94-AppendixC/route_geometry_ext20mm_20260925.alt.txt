Multi-panel route-geometry figure for the optimized 9-point 
extensor BPA routing of the 20 mm vasti design of record 
(Vas_Pam_20mm_Result_20260925.mat). Each panel draws one sagittal
cross-section in the femur frame at a key knee angle: full flexion,
the solver frame immediately before each contact-elimination event,
and full extension. In every panel the magenta circled polyline is
the optimized p1-to-p9 route; clearance envelopes are drawn for the
femur cylinder (gold), femur straight wall (orange), corrected
condyle normal-offset boundary (light orange), and the two tibia
clearance circles (pink and magenta), with purple dashed local bend
radius lines at p2 and p8. A gold square marks the optimized origin
p1 and an orange diamond the optimized distal endpoint pEnd. Panel
titles name the knee angle and which contact points (p3 to p8) are
removed in the following frame; a shared legend column explains all
nine entries.
