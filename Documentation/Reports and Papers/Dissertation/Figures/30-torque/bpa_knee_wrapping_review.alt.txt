Sagittal robot knee schematic with a proximal femur, flexed tibia, and a braided extensor BPA running down the anterior femur and around the knee into a distal tendon. Two dashed contact radii enclose the full bend angle alpha. R labels the radius of the actuator path. The fitted usable-length loss is chi3 times R times alpha times the squared complement of normalized contraction. The figure uses a single circular contact approximation and is not to scale.
