Example of two parallel BPAs actuating a biomimetic knee with crossed links. Total BPA force is 2F20. A moving sheave is supported by n=2 cable parts; transmitted tendon force is total BPA force divided by n, and tendon travel is n times BPA contraction. The reaction magnitude at the BPA origin is total BPA force. An arrow shows the vector reaction at the fixed femoral tackle mount, which includes the cable anchor and right-angle redirect sheave.
