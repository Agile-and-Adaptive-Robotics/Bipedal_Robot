# Pinned-knee extensor torque and cam-assisted extensor route

## Figure 4.9

The dissertation uses the paper's six-panel pinned-extensor figure. The two PDFs have identical contents. Its native dimensions are 500 by 440 pt. Axes span −135° to +41.5° and −2 to 20 N m. Panel C contains the legend. Blue circles mark measured torque, gold circles mark the hybrid calculation, the dashed salmon curve shows the unoptimized model, and the solid magenta curve shows the optimized model. [Paper figure](../../../../../Knee_Torque_Test/Figures/ExtPin_complete_publication.pdf) · [Dissertation figure](../../pinned_extensor_torque.pdf) · [Rendering script](../../../../../Knee_Torque_Test/Figures/Components/torque_plots/draw_plots.py)

Both manuscripts use the same `tests` array in `extensor_data.mat`. Panels A–F are original tests 5, 6, 7, 1, 2, and 8, respectively. The first three were used for fitting and the last three for validation, as reported in Appendix C, Table C12. The geometry and all 113 pairs of measured angle and torque and 113 pairs of hybrid angle and torque come from `ExtPinBPASet.mat`. The model curves use row 32 of the saved extensor identification. [Raw trials](../../../../../../../Testing_Data/2022_02_Festo/ExtPinBPASet.mat) · [Adopted fit](../../../../../../../Testing_Data/2022_02_Festo/minimizeExt10mmX3_results.mat) · [MATLAB input export](export_extensor_data.m)

The original model grid ends at +35°. The paper's continuation replays the 100 original poses in each panel, checks that the wrapping points have released, and extends the models through the last measured angle in panel D. At +41.5° the measured torque is zero and the hybrid calculation gives 3.99185 N m. The corrected model reaches zero by +39.5°. The continuation changes the domain displayed and preserves all experimental records. ## Figure 4.12

The cam data come from `Vas_Pam_20mm_Result_cam_20261014_0416.mat`, the optimization result also used by Appendix C. Panels A and B use the first and last records at the reported angles of −120° and +10°. The plotted route, active-point identities, circle centers and radii, wall and condyle boundaries, bend-radius segments, and bone outlines follow the Appendix C frame transforms and landmark registration. The leader lines separate labels in the full-flexion contact cluster. Panel C is the original photograph of the assembled two-BPA extensor. The full camera frame and orientation are preserved. The eleven-entry key includes the reference bones and the origin and insertion symbols.
