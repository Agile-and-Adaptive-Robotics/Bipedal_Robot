Six plots show pinned-knee extensor torque versus knee angle. All panels span −135° to +50° and −2 to 25 N·m. A horizontal line marks zero torque. The top row shows training tests with resting lengths of 43.6 cm, 43.2 cm with a 22 mm tendon, and 45.7 cm. The bottom row shows validation tests at 40.0 cm, 40.0 cm with a 40 mm tendon, and 46.5 cm. Gold triangles mark the hybrid calculation, blue circles mark measured torque, a dashed line shows the unoptimized prediction, and a solid magenta line shows the optimized prediction. In panel C, the measurements extend to −125° and include −0.0573 N·m at +2°.
