Bilateral full-connectome schematic of the gait2392 spinal-cord network (410
neurons, 201 per leg plus 8 shared, and 376 inputs; the 8 shared cells are
DRIVE, POSTURE, BAL_PF, BAL_DF, BAL_TRK_EXT, BAL_TRK_FLX, BAL_LAT_R, and
BAL_LAT_L), drawn for both legs with no ghost half. A vertical
dashed midline separates the left and right sides, and a pale central
corridor gathers the interleg commissural inhibition wires (F-to-F strong,
E-to-E weak). Each side is organized in labeled tinted layer bands from top
to bottom: descending DRIVE (MLR surrogate), POSTURE, and balance inputs
(BAL_PF/DF sagittal COM PD and BAL_AB/TRK abduct/trunk); RHYTHM GENERATOR
(orange band) with the persistent-sodium RG-E and RG-F half-centers, their
mutual inhibition, and ADAP adaptation cells; PATTERN FORMATION (blue band)
with phase-window cells PF-E1, PF-E2, PF-F1, PF-F2 and their PFA
adaptation interneurons; IN + SENSORS with the Ia, II, and Ib afferent
populations (46 of each per side) and the five RG-E stance-gated IB-EXC
load-sharing interneurons; and MOTONEURON POOLS (light-blue band) with ten
clusters per side (HIP-E x7, HIP-F x4, KNEE-E x4, KNEE-F x4, ANK-PF x9,
ANK-DF x3, HIP-AB x6, HIP-AD x6, TRK-E x1, TRK-F x2) driving their muscle
ellipses. Open circles are neurons, white triangles mark excitatory
synapses, and solid black dots mark inhibitory synapses; wire colors encode
the source (extensor-half drive blue, flexor-half drive orange, other
excitatory green, inhibitory pink, descending and balance light dashed
blue). A gray source note names fitted_walk_params.json (IK/NNLS back-solve
refit) with the live network size, and points to the circuit_core key for
pool abbreviations and member muscles.
