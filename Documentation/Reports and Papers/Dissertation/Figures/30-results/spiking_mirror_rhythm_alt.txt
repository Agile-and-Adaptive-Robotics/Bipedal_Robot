Alt text for spiking_mirror_rhythm.pdf

Two panels over twenty seconds of network-only simulation under a constant
2.5 nanoampere drive. Panel A shows the difference between the right
rhythm-generator extensor and flexor readout voltages in millivolts for
the non-spiking analog network: an irregular square-wave-like signal
alternating over a swing of about 4.8 millivolts between roughly 2.3 and
7.1 millivolts after a larger startup transient, with an average period of
0.399 seconds. Panel B shows the cumulative spike counts of the raw right
extensor and flexor rhythm-generator cells of the spiking mirror as two
staircase traces: the solid orange extensor staircase and the dashed
indigo flexor staircase rise in alternating bursts, adding about 98
extensor and 64 flexor spikes over the twenty seconds and reaching about
98 and 64 by the right edge, with the burst-to-burst alternation regular
at an envelope period of 0.621 seconds. The spiking mirror therefore
self-sustains the same alternating rhythm but at a 55.6 percent longer
period than the analog network at this drive.
