Three columns compare separate inhibitory current pulses to the left extensor rhythm-generator, hip pattern-formation, and hip motor neuron in the same suspended 108-neuron W2L model. Seven aligned rows show actual injected current, paired antagonist RG, PF, and motor-neuron voltages, and hip, knee, and ankle angles. Blue solid and orange dashed neural traces are the named extensor and flexor pathways, respectively. Gray lines show the same unperturbed baseline. Shading marks each 100 ms pulse. The rhythm-generator and pattern-formation pulses visibly alter downstream neural and mechanical signals; the motor-neuron pulse has a smaller effect.
