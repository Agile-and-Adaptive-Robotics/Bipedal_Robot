My 4 step approach to training joint networks for forward walking
1. Picked a CPG which oscillates at ~2x the period of the stepping animal without drive.
2. Using Matlab, trained input connections from afferent feedback (based on expected feedback) to cause transitions at speed of walking animal.
3. Trained output of CPG-PF to MNs to match peaks
4. Trained afferent feedback (

 and 
Ia
) to more closely match desired activation
