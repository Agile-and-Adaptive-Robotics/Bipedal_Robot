## What the FSA scripts calculate

Chapter 6, Section 6.5.1 introduces the functional subnetwork approach. Appendix D, Section D.1.4 gives the right rectus femoris stretch-feedback example. In the s3k connectome a 1 nA input to the Ia sensory neuron brings its voltage to 1 mV and its synaptic activation to 0.2. A maximum conductance of 0.5 µS supplies 0.5 nA at the target motor voltage of 3 mV. That offsets the extra leak as the motor neuron moves from 2.5 to 3.0 mV. The same equilibrium test covered 368 isolated sensory input groups per controller in s3k, w2lvar, and syn6. Separate prescribed-current tests on connected spiking populations explain why the firing-rate calculation includes the receiving population's total conductance. See the production [nonspiking design script](../../../../../Code/MuJoCo_SNS/spinal/fsa_design_20261101/graded_profile.py) and [spiking design script](../../../../../Code/MuJoCo_SNS/spinal/fsa_design_20261101/table2_glif.py). The cited FSA method comes from [Szczecinski, Hunt, and Quinn (2017)](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnbot.2017.00037) and [Szczecinski, Quinn, and Hunt (2020)](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnbot.2020.577804).

The distinct gait inverse calculation estimates recruitment from Subject01 motion and recorded ground forces. The archive-sign replay with the complete joint-follower polynomial selected five right and six left components individually, hence six for both, with 86.6% and 84.2% of activation variance reconstructed by the fitted motor-neuron dynamics. That replay retained legacy hip rotation signs. Applying the six measured hip-axis signs selected six components independently on each side and gave 84.2% right and 84.0% left. The latter result is the one in the dissertation. The reference has one complete stride per side. The rank-six factorizations converged in 1,918 and 122 iterations, and increasing the iteration limit to 60,000 preserved the threshold-crossing ranks. These are in-recording reconstructions. The syn6 forward trials keep their original, saved six-component controller, allowing its actual timing to be compared with the intended timing.

## Why retuning was necessary

Native Gait2392 muscles produced five right and four left unloaded clearances. In matched replays the left count fell to two with either bilateral muscle pair changed, and zero with both. The peak left-toe height fell from 30.5 to 19.0, 17.1, and 6.7 mm. Each mechanical change preserved five right and four left ground contacts, establishing why contact count alone obscured the loss. Nine of the twelve local tuning trials met the original clearance and assistance criteria. Trial 7 recovered five right and four left qualifying swings and was selected for repeating the native cycle count under comparable assistance. The average harness force was 48.28% of body weight, compared with 48.85% with the native muscles. All 7,700 saved samples in this trial reproduced exactly. Chapter 7 treats the paired substitutions and recovered clearance as completed work. Its remaining questions concern cadence, reduced harness assistance, and separating tendon compliance from the active force–velocity relation.
