## A worked example that earns its place

I opened the offline Explorer, selected **Table → Pathway → Ib disynaptic excitation**, obtained six Papers, and opened McCrea et al. (1995). The drawer shows the Cat classification, Ia and Ib tags, links to both disynaptic excitation and inhibition, a curation note, a separate robot/simulation interpretation, and the article DOI. The Paper ID is `recBMsJS4yKuHNuo5`. The two pathway IDs are `recznPoxFR6AVN36u` (excitation) and `recsaUPV6EKpf08tO` (inhibition). Six is a count of linked publications. Reading the original study answers why a single paper belongs to both groups. [Export records](../../../../../SADb_audit/export/sadb_export.json) · [Saved primary abstract](../../../../../SADb_audit/export/grounding.json) · [McCrea et al., 1995](https://physoc.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1113/jphysiol.1995.sp020897)

| Stimulus and recorded target | Condition in the cat | Observed short-latency response |
|---|---|---|
| Plantaris nerve, group I input → medial gastrocnemius motoneurons | At rest | Inhibition |
| Same input and target | Extensor phase of midbrain-evoked fictive locomotion in a decerebrate preparation | Excitation |
| Same input and target | Fictive locomotion in an acute spinal preparation treated with clonidine | Inhibition weakened or disappeared. The short-latency excitation was absent. |

**Use a small functional circuit diagram.** Show the stimulated nerve and recorded motor population once, then identify the sign measured under each condition. Fictive extension is a phase of the experimental rhythm. The stimulus recruited group I fibers, and the authors showed that Ia afferents could contribute. Response latency supports a disynaptic interpretation. The intervening cell’s identity and individual synaptic weights remain undetermined. This supports testing a phase- and preparation-dependent net response. Calling the sketch an anatomical connectome, labeling the input Ib-only, or drawing two separately measured synapses would overstate the experiment. Chapter 6 provides a separate implemented example: an extensor Ia input pulse recruited the knee extensor motor neuron, increased force, and extended the model knee. This offers a defensible handoff from candidate rule to experimental tools.
