| Plantaris nerve, group I input → medial gastrocnemius motoneurons | At rest | Inhibition |

**Use a small functional circuit diagram.** Show the stimulated nerve and recorded motor population once, then identify the sign measured under each condition. Fictive extension is a phase of the experimental rhythm. The stimulus recruited group I fibers, and the authors showed that Ia afferents could contribute. Response latency supports a disynaptic interpretation. The intervening cell’s identity and individual synaptic weights remain undetermined. This supports testing a phase- and preparation-dependent net response. Calling the sketch an anatomical connectome, labeling the input Ib-only, or drawing two separately measured synapses would overstate the experiment. Chapter 6 provides a separate implemented example: an extensor Ia input pulse recruited the knee extensor motor neuron, increased force, and extended the model knee. This offers a defensible handoff from candidate rule to experimental tools.
