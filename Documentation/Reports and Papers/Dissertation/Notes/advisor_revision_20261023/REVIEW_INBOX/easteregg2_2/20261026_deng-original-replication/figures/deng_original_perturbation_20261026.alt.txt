Alt text: Four stacked time-series panels spanning 1.6 to 3.6 seconds, each
showing a neuron membrane-voltage trace in millivolts from minus 100 to 0.
Every panel overlays an unperturbed baseline trace in blue with a stimulated
trace in red, with a light gray stimulus band from 2.0 to 2.5 seconds and a
dashed vertical line at 2.1 seconds. Panel A, left rhythm-generator flexor:
the red trace fires an extra burst exactly at 2.0 seconds where the blue
baseline stays quiet until 2.4 seconds. Panel B, left rhythm-generator
extensor: the red trace fires early at 2.1 seconds versus 2.6 seconds in the
baseline, then both traces burst rhythmically at the same rate. Panel C, left
rhythm-generator extensor under a knee-level stimulus instead: the red and
blue traces are nearly identical. Panel D, left knee extensor motoneuron under
the knee-level stimulus held for the full half-second window: the red trace is
strongly suppressed, sitting tens of millivolts below the blue baseline during
the window. All voltage traces are square bursts clipped at 0 millivolts.
