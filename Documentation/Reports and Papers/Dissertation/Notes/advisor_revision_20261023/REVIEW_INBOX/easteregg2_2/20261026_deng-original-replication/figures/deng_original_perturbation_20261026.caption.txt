published stimulation protocol, in its original engine. In every panel the
blue trace is the unperturbed baseline and the red trace is the stimulated
run; the shaded band marks \SIrange{2}{2.5}{\second}, and the dashed line
marks \qty{2.1}{\second}, the paper-exact stimulus end (the stimulus in
panels (A)--(C) ends there, while the local knee-level stimulus in panel (D)
is held for the full band).
(A)~Left rhythm-generator flexor population: the \qty{2}{\nano\ampere}
rhythm-generator stimulus triggers an additional short burst beginning at
stimulus onset (\qty{2.000}{\second}, against \qty{2.433}{\second} in the
baseline). (B)~Left rhythm-generator extensor population, same run: the
extensor rhythm continues through the stimulus (early onset at
\qty{2.124}{\second}, against \qty{2.589}{\second} baseline) and recovers its
period. (C)~Left rhythm-generator extensor population with the same current
applied instead at the knee pattern-formation flexor: the trace is
indistinguishable from baseline (post-stimulus phase
$-\qty{2.3 \pm 2.4}{\percent}$). (D)~Left knee extensor motoneuron when the
knee pattern-formation flexor is held for the full \qty{0.5}{\second} window:
the local effect appears (in-window mean \qty{22.3}{\milli\volt} below
baseline), while panel (C) shows the rhythm generator unaffected. Voltages
are neuron membrane voltages clipped at \qty{0}{\milli\volt} by the
integrate-and-fire element; positive current is depolarizing.
