The redesigned cam-extensor route was tested at nine isometric data measurements, and the two-BPA flexor route at fifteen. Both test sets span knee angles from \ang{-119} to \ang{5}. The test-specific analysis scripts transform the measured load-cell wrench to the moving knee center and evaluate model predictions at each measured angle and pressure. Figures~\ref{fig:extensor_physical_final} and~\ref{fig:flexor_physical_final} compare these reductions with the human-reference torques and the optimized \qty{620}{\kPa} curves.

On the cam-extensor route, the nine data measurements reduce to knee-extension torques of \qty{32.52}{\newton\meter} at \ang{-119}, \qty{38.91}{\newton\meter} at \ang{-86.5}, \qty{47.12}{\newton\meter} at \ang{-61}, \qty{50.50}{\newton\meter} at \ang{-43}, \qty{51.17}{\newton\meter} at \ang{-30}, \qty{50.50}{\newton\meter} at \ang{-26.2}, \qty{48.71}{\newton\meter} at \ang{-22}, \qty{35.90}{\newton\meter} at \ang{-1}, and \qty{29.02}{\newton\meter} at \ang{5}, at supply pressures of \qtyrange{256}{488}{\kPa}. All nine data measurements meet or exceed the maximum-isometric vasti target at the measured angles, with margins from \qty{0.24}{\newton\meter} to \qty{3.52}{\newton\meter}, and the corrected prediction of the cam design evaluated at the measured angles and pressures is exceeded at eight of the nine angles, by up to \qty{13.75}{\newton\meter}, and falls below the data once, by \qty{2.06}{\newton\meter} at \ang{-86.5}; the pressure-specific force-model prediction is distinct from the hybrid calculation, which requires the measured muscle length and moment arm that this test did not record. Agreement with the pressure-specific prediction therefore remains uneven, particularly for the flexor at intermediate angles, and the tests support the redesigned routes' torque capacity while identifying prediction errors that remain to be explained. Since pressure varied between data measurements, the measured points do not constitute a full-pressure envelope at every angle.
