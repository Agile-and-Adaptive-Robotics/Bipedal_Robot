A subsequent transmission study re-solved actuator sizes for each tackle configuration at five anatomical muscle sites. A two-BPA pair through a two-part tackle preserves net actuator force while doubling insertion travel; increasing travel can extend the angle range over which a route meets its torque requirement. These calculations use the moving-force-point routing, with the adjacent joint fixed for the biarticular evaluation planes; they do not establish simultaneous performance across both joints. Physical verification of the pulley configurations remains future work.
