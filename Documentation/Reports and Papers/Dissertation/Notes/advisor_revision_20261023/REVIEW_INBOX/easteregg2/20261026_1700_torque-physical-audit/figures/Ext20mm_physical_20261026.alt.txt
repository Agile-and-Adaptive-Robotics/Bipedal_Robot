Single-panel line and scatter plot of knee-extension torque versus knee angle from -125 to +15 degrees. A magenta solid curve (cam design of record at 620 kPa) rises from about 105 N m at -120 degrees to a broad hump of roughly 66-76 N m between -60 and -20 degrees, then falls to about 47 N m near +5 degrees. A black dotted human-target curve (vasti) lies below it everywhere, about 29 N m at -119 degrees rising to about 50 N m near -40 degrees and falling to 28 N m at +5 degrees. Nine indigo filled circles (measured data measurements, area scaling with gauge pressure 256-488 kPa) sit between 29 and 52 N m and meet or exceed the black target at every measured angle; nine gold circles (corrected model evaluated at each measured angle and pressure) run below the indigo points at eight of the nine angles. Y axis labeled M_k in newton meters, x axis labeled theta_k in degrees; legend at upper right.
