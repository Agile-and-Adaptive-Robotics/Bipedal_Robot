Years later, I split the shared RG into one RG for each side and connected them through commissural pathways. I also extended sensory feedback to the RG and PF layers and added foot-contact feedback. This later bilateral-RG extension is referred to here as the Walker Two-Layer (W2L) model; it is distinct from the original controller shown in Figure~\ref{fig:animatlab_model_architecture}(B). Section~\ref{sec:w2l_perturbation} reports its coupled-model tests; further circuit schematics and implementation records are provided in Appendix~\ref{app:simulation_verification}.
