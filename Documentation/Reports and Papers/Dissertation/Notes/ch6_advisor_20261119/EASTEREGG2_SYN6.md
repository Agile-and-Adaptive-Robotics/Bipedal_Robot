# The syn6 walking-reference overlay and defense workflow

## Which trial produced the plot?

The corrected left/right overlay comes from a completed, 16 s, suspended Gait2392 trial on easteregg2. The syn6 circuit used live afferents and a modified pattern-formation (PF) pathway. A predictive knee relay shut off the S2 recruitment window and held it closed until the next extensor rhythm-generator (RG) onset on that side. The four later S3 variants whose folders surround the figure each stopped at the protective knee limit. The training record identifies the final figure by its corrected side order and Ong legend. ## What was held fixed, and what failed?

The saved configuration uses the 92-actuator Gait2392 model with a shared-parameter muscle force law, frozen motor synapses, live length, velocity, and force signals, and normalized flexion and extension commands of 0.15 per side. The support spring and frontal restraints remained active. The mean spring force was 768.70 N from 5 s to the end of the force record, or 99.865% of the evaluator’s 769.738 N body-weight denominator. Explicit upward support was zero, and ground contact was disabled. The run began at −7° hip, −10° knee, and 0° ankle. Its 150 ms knee-protection relay stayed enabled. The adaptive PF circuit completed handover at 6.242 s.
