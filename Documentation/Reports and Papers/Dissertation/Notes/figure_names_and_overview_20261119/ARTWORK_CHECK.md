# Visible dates in dissertation figures

All 81 graphics included by the working manuscript were reviewed. None contains a build date, export timestamp, dated run identifier, or revision date in its visible artwork. Each of the eleven assets with a date in its former filename retains the same SHA-256 under its new name. The review required zero artwork or generator edits.

The inventory came from active `includegraphics` commands. It comprises 62 PDFs, 8 EPS files, 9 PNGs, and 2 JPEGs. The EPS checks used the PDF conversions loaded by LaTeX. I inspected the rendered artwork of all 81, searched the text objects in the 70 PDF and EPS renderings, and ran Windows English OCR on each rendered image. Seventeen of the PDF and EPS renderings contain raster components. I examined the SADb screenshot header, paper details, and map at higher magnification. The SHA-256 of the sorted list of the 81 content hashes, one per line, is `64b5bbd99279d9aa5db2b732ab34f7c3eeaad48d713125ad0af6486dc63e22ba`. The same fingerprint was obtained after the eleven filename changes.
