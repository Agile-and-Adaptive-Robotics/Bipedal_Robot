Isometric render of the converted Gait2392 robot-body musculoskeletal model (OpenSim
source converted to MuJoCo; the conversion documented in the dissertation). The model
is shown standing at its saved reference pose on a white ground, viewed from a raised
three-quarter angle (camera approximately 30 degrees above horizontal, 45 degrees of
azimuth). The skeleton is rendered in shaded gray: skull, vertebral column, rib cage,
pelvis, and both legs (the model has no arms). Ninety-two muscle paths are drawn as
thin deep-red polylines following the bones — densest across the pelvis, hip, and
thigh, spanning the hip, knee, and ankle joints, and continuing down to the feet.
Straight-line segments kink at wrapping and via points. A soft gray shadow falls
beneath the feet. The background is pure white. Rendered offscreen with MuJoCo 2.3.7
at 1440 x 2160 px, 300 dpi, 2x supersampled.
