16:+Physical validation of the redesigned extensor route is under way on the rebuilt cam route with the \qty{20}{\mm} actuator, and the first full angle sweep is complete. An initial three-point hold near full extension on the earlier direct route measured knee torque of \qty{1.163}{\newton\meter} at \ang{10}, \qty{7.849}{\newton\meter} at \ang{11}, and \qty{23.437}{\newton\meter} at \ang{10}, against hung loads of \qtylist{3.7;25.2;76}{\newton} at gauge pressures of \qtylist{388.5;388;592}{\kPa}, with the measured wrench carried to the moving knee center by the same adjoint transform as the identification data. On the rebuilt cam route the sweep then held six isometric points from deep flexion to extension: \qty{116}{\newton} hung at \ang{-119} and \qty{256}{\kPa}, \qty{168}{\newton} at \ang{-61} and \qty{425}{\kPa}, \qty{181}{\newton} at \ang{-43} and \qty{435}{\kPa}, \qty{180.2}{\newton} at \ang{-26.2} and \qty{437}{\kPa}, \qty{181.5}{\newton} at \ang{-30} and \qty{420}{\kPa}, and \qty{100}{\newton} at \ang{5} and \qty{480}{\kPa}, each with its load-cell angle recorded in the tibia frame.
