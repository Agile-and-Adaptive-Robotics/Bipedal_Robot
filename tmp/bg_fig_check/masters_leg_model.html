2.2 Muscle Paths Addition of muscles to the model is guided by GaitBody2392. OpenSim attachment points are kept the same, except the via points which are replaced by fixed points. Bipedal robot PAM attachment points are modified to accommodate the contraction limits of Festo PAM - and to try to accommodate 3D geometry realities (i.e. including avoiding interference with other actuators) - while also trying to maintain torques.
