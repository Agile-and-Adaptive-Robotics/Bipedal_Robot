# Animal: Dog

4 papers in the corpus.

- **Stark 2020** — [A three-dimensional musculoskeletal model of the dog](https://doi.org/10.1101/2020.07.16.205856)  
  - animals: Dog  
  - A detailed, scalable 3D dog musculoskeletal model (84 degrees of freedom, 134 muscles) whose inverse-dynamics and static-optimization pipeline recovers the three forelimb synergy groups of a walking beagle with good activation agreement, providing a simulation base for studying joint loading and neuromuscular control in quadruped locomotion.  
  - *Robot/sim:* Use the 84-DOF, 134-muscle dog model as a simulation plant; replicate the inverse-dynamics, static-optimization, and hierarchical-clustering synergy pipeline, then test neural locomotion controllers against the three extracted forelimb synergies.
- **Scharzenberger 2019** — [Design of a Canine Inspired Quadruped Robot as a Platform for Synthetic Neural Network Control](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-24741-6_20)  
  - animals: Dog  
  - Computational model study. DoggyDeux features a fully 3D printed frame, 24 braided pneumatic actuators (BPAs) that drive four 3-DOF limbs in an- tagonistic extensor-flexor pairs, and an electrical system that allows it to respond to commands from a SNN comprised of central pattern genera- tors (CPGs).
- **Hunt 2017** — [Development and Training of a Neural Controller for Hind Leg Walking in a Dog Robot](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnbot.2017.00018)  
  - animals: Dog, Mammals  
  - An automatic parameter-setting method for synthetic nervous systems of non-spiking leaky integrator neurons on physical robots: motor-neuron activations required for stable walking are computed first from a model of the system, then network parameters are tuned so the circuit produces those activations under expected sensory feedback - successfully yielding adaptive rear-leg walking in an artificial-muscle dog robot and supporting the validity of current mammalian locomotion circuit models.  
  - *Robot/sim:* Apply the two-stage tuning recipe - solve for MN activations that produce stable walking, then fit SNS parameters so the circuit generates those activations under expected sensory feedback - and ablate individual sensory pathways to test their necessity for adaptive walking.
- **Sherrington 1910** — [Flexion-reflex of the limb, crossed extension-reflex, and reflex stepping and standing.](https://doi.org/10.1113/jphysiol.1910.sp001362)  
  - animals: Cat, Dog · pathways: Cutaneous flexor excitation  
  - Defined the flexion-reflex as a protective type-reflex of the whole limb and its companion crossed extension-reflex in the spinal cat and dog, and showed that reflex stepping fractionates them by phase: the extensor phase of one limb is reinforced by a crossed extension-reflex driven by the opposite limb's flexion, with stimulus-dependent reversal (Umkehr) marking the turning points of the step.
