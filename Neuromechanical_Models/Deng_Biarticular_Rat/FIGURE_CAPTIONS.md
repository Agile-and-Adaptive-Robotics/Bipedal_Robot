# Native baseline figure descriptions

## Native_baseline_neural_joint.png

Suggested diagnostic caption: Left-leg neural and joint activity from the imported public biarticular rat precursor in AnimatLab. Extensor and flexor traces are shown at the rhythm-generation, hip pattern-formation, and hip motor-neuron levels, followed by hip, knee, and ankle source coordinates. The 3.345–4.8-second interval contains approximately three left-leg cycles from the same native recording. The pelvis is fixed. This panel does not establish bilateral alternation; the right RG remains latched in the corresponding source baseline.

Alt text: Six aligned time plots show alternating blue solid extensor and orange dashed flexor activity at RG, hip PF, and hip MN levels, with periodic left hip, knee, and ankle coordinates below. The knee flexes during each cycle. The plotted interval is about one and a half seconds. Source angle signs and zeros are retained.

## Native_biarticular_activity.png

Suggested diagnostic caption: Left BFP, RF, and GA motor-neuron voltages and root y position during the same native suspended rat-model baseline. The constant root position is imposed by the author's frozen pelvis, rather than achieved balance.

Alt text: Three panels show periodic motor-neuron voltages for the left-leg BFP, RF, and GA channels. The fourth panel shows an unchanged zero root y coordinate because the pelvis is fixed.

## Native_bilateral_RG_diagnostic.png

Suggested diagnostic caption: Left and right RG activity on matching voltage axes. Left RG oscillation is present, whereas the original public model's right RG remains effectively constant. This source baseline fails a bilateral-rhythm criterion.

Alt text: Two aligned RG panels share a minus 64 to minus 54 millivolt scale. Left extensor and flexor signals alternate; right extensor and flexor signals are nearly flat.

All three panels are measured native results from the public 2020 precursor. They are not final-2023 model replications, MuJoCo coupled results, or ground-walking demonstrations. Joint coordinates have not been anatomically calibrated to extension-neutral markers. Raw trace provenance and exact numerical diagnostics are in NATIVE_BASELINE_RESULTS.json.
