- The winner's rhythm is AFFERENT-SUSTAINED at this operating point: with
  zero heel/toe/load input both RG half-centers sit on the NaP plateau
  (~5.1 mV, no bursts; probe1) — the documented s3k-lineage property. The
  contact script inside the model demonstrates the reset-driven rhythm
  the production gait uses.

  contra_* crossed edges (no_cross=1) and the MuJoCo body are OUT of the
  slice; their ports are simply not driven. POST_<muscle> MN biases
  default to 0 (production values come from the runner's MuJoCo standing
  solve; they cannot affect the RG/PF rhythm).
- The 4 stacked IBEXC->LBIN edges (audit N1, reported-not-fixed) are
  merged into one 2.0 uS synapse — algebraically exact (parallel
  conductances sum).
- Full-net vs slice MN levels differ (2.902 vs 2.247 mV) because in the
  full winner every Renshaw cell is inhibited by ~46 same-side pools;
  the 2-pool slice sits lower in BOTH numpy and Simulink identically.
