## What is missing for a BPA robot rather than ideal tensions

The 54 source channels are fixed-gain reference actuators with a 30 ms filter: tension = peak capacity × normalized state. Their 5% state therefore imposes 5% peak tension in that ideal model. They have no force-length/velocity/passive law; this is not a verified physiological 5% activation or measured BPA coactivation. Do not invent a source-specific physiological floor for the 52 reference channels.

Only the vas_med pair has an explicit virtual pressure configuration: single 20 mm diameter, rest length 0.2667809648 m, maximum contraction fraction 0.25, series stiffness 200,000 N/m, pressure cap 620 kPa, fill/vent constants 40/60 ms, and transport delay 4 ms. Fixed nonmuscle spans differ by side; the remote path offset is 0.3167809648 m. These are documented virtual engineering assumptions. They are not a mounted actuator, valve identification, or dynamic/hysteretic calibration. The same frozen configuration fails its contraction/force domain at the newer near-closed stance. Do not resize it after that failure and call it the original model.

Still required for a hardware-representative assignment: actual sleeve/rest lengths and braid/end-fitting geometry; mount/guide/ pulley or cable topology and mechanical advantage; stroke and packaging clearance over ROM; route-specific pressure–force–length data; valve flow/deadband and pressure dynamics with tubing/compressor limits; dynamic contraction/eccentric behavior and hysteresis if modeled; compliance/damping and hardware mass/inertia. Body-fixed virtual sites currently assume rigid massless brackets and need not lie on CAD surfaces. Moving/conditional paths must retain complete Jacobians and velocities, not just moving visualization lines. Feet welded in the old model remove leverage from 20 ankle/toe channels; enabling ankle/subtalar/MTP joints is a distinct articulated design requiring real joint/mount data.
